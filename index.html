Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you want to know before getting started. Still unsure? Just WhatsApp us.

Do I need any technical knowledge to work with Webify?

+

Not at all. We handle absolutely everything, from buying your domain name and setting up hosting to designing, building, and launching your full online presence. You just need to tell us about your business and we do the rest. No computer skills required.

How long does it take before my business is live online?

+

Most projects are completed and live within 3 to 5 business days from the day we start. We'll give you a clear timeline upfront so you know exactly what to expect. Complex website projects may take a few days longer.

Is the pricing once-off or monthly?

+

All our setup packages are once-off payments. The only ongoing costs are things like domain registration (approximately R200/year) and web hosting (from R100/month) — which we'll explain clearly before you commit to anything. No surprises.

Can you help my business even if I'm not in Tembisa?

+

Absolutely! We serve businesses across all of South Africa. Everything is handled remotely over WhatsApp and email — no in-person meetings required. We've worked with clients from Cape Town to Limpopo.

What support do I get after my project is done?

+

Every client gets a thorough handover so you understand everything that was set up for you. Growth and Pro clients get dedicated support periods included. And for any quick questions, you can always WhatsApp us — we respond within 24 hours.

I already have a website but it's not working. Can you help?

+

Yes! Send us the link to your current website on WhatsApp and we'll review it for free and give you honest advice on what's holding it back and what we can do to improve it.
