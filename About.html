Where It All Began

A simple question:
"Why can't I find local
businesses online?"

Webify was born out of frustration and purpose. Our founder noticed that most businesses especially the ones situated in the townships and semi rural areas struggle to grow online due factors like high cost and lack of access to digital tools.

We started small. One laptop, one client, one WhatsApp conversation at a time. Today we've helped 15+ businesses to get appear online, get cliets, and grow, and we're just getting started.

Every feature we build, every service we offer, and every decision we make is driven by one belief: every business deserves a premium online presence, regardless of budget or background.
